How It Works

Three steps. Milliseconds to execute.

01

Vector Embedding

Title + description are combined and passed through the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model locally — no external API call. Output: a 384-dimensional float array.

02

$vectorSearch

MongoDB Atlas finds the 3 most conceptually similar ideas using HNSW approximate nearest-neighbor search with cosine similarity metric.

03

Risk Verdict

Similarity scores are interpreted into a risk level (HIGH / MEDIUM / CROSS-DOMAIN / LOW) and a uniqueness percentage is computed as 1 − top_score.

04

Save & Track

If novel enough, save your idea directly to Atlas. It then becomes part of the dataset — sharpening future validations for everyone.

About Validra

Sun-Baked
Precision

An AI-powered startup idea similarity checker built on MongoDB Atlas Vector Search, local transformer embeddings, and a clean Node.js/Express API.

Vector Model
all-MiniLM-L6-v2
384-dimensional sentence embeddings via @xenova/transformers (fully local — no external API costs)
Database
MongoDB Atlas
$vectorSearch (HNSW) for similarity · Atlas Search for real-time autocomplete · unique index on title
Auth
JWT + bcryptjs
User accounts stored in MongoDB. Passwords hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds). Tokens expire in 7 days.

Ready to

Your idea passed validation. Now follow these steps to register and launch your startup globally. Each step links to official resources — no guesswork.

01

Choose Your Business Structure

Decide how your company will be legally organized. This determines liability, taxation, and fundraising capability.

Sole Proprietorship Simplest — you are the business. No separation of personal/business liability.
LLC (Limited Liability Company) Best for most startups. Protects personal assets. Flexible taxation.
C-Corporation ✨ Recommended for VC Required if you plan to raise venture capital. Delaware C-Corp is the global standard.
LLP (Limited Liability Partnership) Good for co-founder teams. Popular in UK, India, Singapore.
02

Register Your Company

Officially incorporate in your chosen jurisdiction. Here are popular options worldwide:

03

Protect Your Intellectual Property

Before going public, file for IP protection in your core markets.

☐ Trademark — Register your brand name and logo. Use WIPO Madrid System for global coverage.
☐ Patent (Optional) — If your tech is novel, file a provisional patent. Use USPTO or EPO.
☐ Domain Name — Secure your .com, .io, or country-specific domain via Namecheap or Google Domains.
☐ NDA Template — Have a mutual NDA ready for early conversations. Free templates on DocuSign.
04

Set Up Financial Infrastructure

Get your banking and payments in order before you start collecting revenue.

☐ Business Bank Account — Mercury, Brex, or your local bank. Keep personal and business funds separate.
☐ Tax ID / EIN — Apply for an Employer Identification Number (US) or equivalent in your country.
☐ Accounting Software — Set up QuickBooks, Xero, or Zoho Books from day one.
☐ Payment Gateway — Integrate Stripe or Razorpay for collecting payments globally.
05

Build Your MVP & Go Live

Ship something real. You don't need perfection — you need validation from real users.

☐ Landing Page — Use Carrd, Webflow, or build custom. Capture emails before launch.
☐ MVP — Build the smallest version that solves the core problem. Ship in weeks, not months.
☐ Launch Platform — Post on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and relevant subreddits.
☐ Analytics — Set up Plausible or Google Analytics to track traction from day one.
